Crop Database

This crop database contains information about different crops produced in Zimbabwe.

Maize

Maize is one of the major cereal crops grown in Zimbabwe. Rainfed maize is mainly planted at the beginning of the summer rainy season, while production practices vary according to rainfall and farming conditions.

Maize growing information
Growing season Planting period Harvest period water requirement
Summer rainy season November to December April to June 500 to 800 millimetres

Wheat

Wheat is an important cereal crop in Zimbabwe and is mostly produced during the winter season under irrigation. Wheat is planted during the cooler months and harvested after the winter season.

Wheat growing information
Growing season Planting period Harvest period water requirement
Winter season April to May October to November 450 to 650 millimetres

Sorghum

Sorghum is a drought-tolerant cereal crop grown in Zimbabwe, particularly in areas where rainfall is lower or less reliable. It is commonly grown during the summer rainy season.

Sorghum growing information
Growing season Planting period Harvest period water requirement
Summer rainy season November to January March to April 450 to 650 millimetres

Agricultural Alerts

Low Rainfall and Soil Moisture Alert

Farmers in areas experiencing below-normal rainfall should monitor soil moisture and conserve available water.

Issue date: 9 September 2026

Crop Pest Monitoring Alert

Farmers are encouraged to regularly inspect crops for signs of insect pests such as stem borers in maize and other crop damage. Identifying pests early can help farmers take control measures before infestations become serious.

Issue date: 9 September 2026
